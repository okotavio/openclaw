Privacy policy for Luna

Effective 31 October 2026.

Data access

Luna accesses Google data only from accounts explicitly authorized by its owner. The configured permissions may include read-only Gmail, Calendar, and Drive access.

Use

Data is used only to answer the owner's requests and provide scheduled personal-assistant functions. Luna does not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials and retrieved data are stored on the owner's private server. Data is sent to the AI provider selected by the owner only when needed to generate a response. No other sharing is intended.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the app owner through the support email configured in Google Cloud.
